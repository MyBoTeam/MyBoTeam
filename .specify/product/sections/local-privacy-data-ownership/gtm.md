## 11.5 Go-to-Market Strategy

**Purpose**: Define how the local-first trust posture should be communicated externally.

### 11.5.1 Launch Phases

#### Phase 1: Practical Trust

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Audience** | Mainstream users evaluating privacy-sensitive AI tools |
| **Goal** | Make local-first control understandable and reassuring |
| **Entry Criteria** | Clear trust language and explicit boundaries |
| **Success Metrics** | Better setup continuation and trust perception |
| **Exit Criteria** | Users continue after trust-sensitive setup points |

#### Phase 2: Trusted Expansion

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Audience** | Returning users connecting more services |
| **Goal** | Show that more capability does not weaken ownership |
| **Entry Criteria** | Stable boundary and permission experience |
| **Success Metrics** | Retention after connector or provider setup |
| **Exit Criteria** | Trust remains a reason to stay |

#### Phase 3: Strategic Differentiation

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Audience** | Users comparing local-first and cloud-default assistants |
| **Goal** | Make trust a durable differentiator |
| **Entry Criteria** | Credible product proof and retention |
| **Success Metrics** | Positive trust-driven product perception |
| **Exit Criteria** | Trust contributes to sustained product preference |

#### Phase 4: Bundle-Compatible Trust

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Audience** | Repeat users considering deeper capabilities |
| **Goal** | Preserve trust while introducing future monetization |
| **Success Metrics** | Bundle interest without trust erosion |

### 11.5.2 Pricing Strategy

| Tier | Price | Includes | Target Segment |
|------|-------|----------|----------------|
| **Free** | $0 | Local-first core product and trust posture | Broad simple users |
| **Curated Bundles** | Future pricing | Additional workflow value that preserves local control | Returning users |
| **Enterprise** | Not current scope | No present commitment | Not current target |

### 11.5.3 Key Messaging

**For mainstream users:** "Your assistant works on your machine, under your control."

**For privacy-aware users:** "Connect outside services only when you choose to."

**For future repeat users:** "More capability does not require surrendering ownership."

### 11.5.4 Success Metrics by Phase

| Phase | Adoption Target | Engagement Target | Revenue Target |
|-------|----------------|-------------------|----------------|
| Practical Trust | Higher trust-sensitive activation | Continued early use | None |
| Trusted Expansion | More connected-service users | Better retention | None |
| Strategic Differentiation | Stronger trust-based preference | Repeat usage | Pre-revenue validation |
| Bundle-Compatible Trust | Bundle interest | Bundle reuse | Future commercial target |

### 11.5.5 Channel Strategy

| Channel | Purpose | Target | Investment |
|---------|---------|--------|------------|
