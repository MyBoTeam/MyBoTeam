## 4. Goals/Objectives

**Purpose**: Define what success looks like for the trust model.

### 4.1 Primary Goal

Make users feel that MyBoTeam works for them locally, not on behalf of a hidden service boundary.

### 4.2 Technical Goal

Keep secrets, settings, and task data local while exposing explicit edges where external providers or connectors are user-chosen dependencies.

### 4.3 Business Goal

Use local-first trust as a durable differentiator that supports adoption and future commercialization without a hosted backend requirement.

### 4.4 Goals Traced to PDRs

| Goal | Type | PDR | Category |
|------|------|-----|----------|
| Practical local trust | Primary | PDR-005 | Trust Model |
| Human-readable privacy positioning | Primary | PDR-001 | Positioning |
| Business independence from hosted backend | Business | PDR-008 | Business Model |

### 4.5 Success Definition

**We will know we've succeeded when:**

- Users understand that their data and credentials remain local by default.
- Provider and connector boundaries feel explicit rather than surprising.
- Trust messaging supports adoption instead of sounding abstract or legalistic.
