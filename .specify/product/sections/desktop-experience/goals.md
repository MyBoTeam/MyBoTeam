## 4. Goals/Objectives

**Purpose**: Define the success criteria for the overall desktop product shell.

### 4.1 Primary Goal

Make the first-run desktop experience simple enough for mainstream users to get value quickly.

### 4.2 Technical Goal

Preserve a broad configurable desktop surface without letting it destabilize onboarding or current product clarity.

### 4.3 Business Goal

Use a polished desktop control center as the foundation for retention, repeat usage, and future premium expansion.

### 4.4 Goals Traced to PDRs

| Goal | Type | PDR | Category |
|------|------|-----|----------|
| Guided simple-mode onboarding | Primary | PDR-009 | User Experience |
| Simple-user friendly product language | Primary | PDR-001 | Positioning |
| Future-ready configurable shell | Business | PDR-008 | Business Model |

### 4.5 Success Definition

**We will know we've succeeded when:**

- New users can start useful work before diving into advanced settings.
- Advanced users can still access deeper controls when they need them.
- Current locale and feature claims stay aligned with shipped reality.
