## 4. Goals/Objectives

**Purpose**: Define what success looks like for built-in action capabilities.

### 4.1 Primary Goal

Enable the assistant to complete useful tasks across connectors and browsers without forcing users to operate raw tool catalogs.

### 4.2 Technical Goal

Package skills, connector auth, MCP tools, and automation flows as stable first-party capability families available to tasks.

### 4.3 Business Goal

Use built-in action capability to drive clear product differentiation and future bundle potential.

### 4.4 Goals Traced to PDRs

| Goal | Type | PDR | Category |
|------|------|-----|----------|
| Outcome-first built-in capabilities | Primary | PDR-004 | Product Capability |
| Action-based differentiation | Primary | PDR-007 | Differentiation |
| Future bundle leverage | Business | PDR-008 | Business Model |

### 4.5 Success Definition

**We will know we've succeeded when:**

- Users complete tasks that require external actions.
- Built-in capabilities feel like product value, not hidden demos.
- Mainstream users benefit without needing to learn MCP jargon.
